This project was part of the display design for a Bstroy Flea Market, powered by Adidas, for Atlanta's (404) Day. I was tasked with creating renderings for floating holographic fan displays. I used a combination of [EndlessTools](https://endlesstools.io/), Blender, TouchDesigner, and Premiere Pro. The event with Adidas was postponed, however, the graphics were used at an event launching their denim program in April. The final render of the logo animations can be seen below.
