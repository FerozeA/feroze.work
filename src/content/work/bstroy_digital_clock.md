This is a product I designed that has the appearance of a digital scale, while serving the functionality of an ashtray and clock. I saw this concept in napkin-sketch form on the instagram of one of the founders of Bstroy, a neo-native design house based out of Brooklyn. I reached out knowing I had to be a part of the develoipment. Using Fusion360, I designed the first concept. After 40+ revisions, we landed on the version seen below.

After designing the outward facing components, I created a document detailing all of the product specifications including materials, metal finish, clock functionality, menu logic, tactility of the interface, and power.

This project is currently in the sampling stage.
